Profile

Neuroscientist and clinical psychologist with more than 20 years of experience in cellular and systems electrophysiology, experimental neuroscience, neurological disease models, neurodevelopmental disorders, quantitative neuroscience, and university teaching. Research spans neuronal excitability, synaptic plasticity, epilepsy, cerebellar and hippocampal physiology, neuroinflammation and microglia, metabolic and mitochondrial mechanisms, cognition and memory, and computational phenotyping.

h-index 18 in current application materials, with sustained recent output across cellular, systems, neuroimmune, metabolic and computational neuroscience.

International training

Germany · Mexico · International clinical training

Postdoctoral research in Heidelberg and Münster, with clinical training in psychiatry and psychological medicine and subsequent postgraduate training.

Research

Cellular & Systems Neurophysiology

In vivo and in vitro electrophysiology; intrinsic neuronal excitability, ion channels, synaptic integration, long-term plasticity and circuit-state transitions.

Epilepsy & Neural Plasticity

Experimental epilepsy and mechanisms linking seizures, neuronal plasticity, cognition and memory, including cerebellar and hippocampal physiology.

Neuroimmune Mechanisms

Microglial biology, neuroinflammation and neurodegeneration, including interactions between cellular signaling and neurological disease.

Computational Neuroscience

Python-based quantitative workflows, machine learning, multivariate phenotyping, dimensionality reduction, statistical modeling and interpretable approaches to multidimensional biological data.
